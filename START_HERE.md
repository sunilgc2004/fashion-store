# START HERE

## You already have the shop running on your PC: just get the new design
1. Extract this ZIP anywhere (for example the Desktop). You get a folder called `fashion-store`.
2. Open it, then open `web`. Copy the folder named `src`.
3. Go to your working project (the folder that contains `server` and `web`), open `web`, and paste.
   Click "Replace the files in the destination".
4. Keep your two windows running (`npm run dev` in `server` and in `web`). Refresh http://localhost:5173 (Ctrl+F5).
Do NOT replace your `server` folder: it holds your `.env` settings and database history.

## Starting on a new computer, or deploying online
Follow RUN_AND_DEPLOY.md.
